# Rice bowl prop

`dafeiyu-rice-bowl-no-logo.svg` is the renderer's rice bowl prop: a white bowl with a gold rim, rice grains and steam, on a transparent 400 × 400 canvas.

The SVG was generated with DeepSeek and edited with Codex to remove the separate whale logo group. The bowl, rice and steam geometry are retained. It contains no DeepSeek logo and does not indicate endorsement by DeepSeek.
